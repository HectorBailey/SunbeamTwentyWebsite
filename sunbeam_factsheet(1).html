SUNBEAM TWENTY TOURER

Vintage British Motor Car • 1934

About This Unique Car

This remarkable automobile has lived a fascinating ninety-one years since its creation in 1934. It is the only Sunbeam Twenty tourer ever produced — a true one-off, specially ordered by the Fry family through John Croall & Sons.

After being laid up in Scotland from 1939 to 1958, it was brought back to life and has since covered an estimated 500,000 miles. Remarkably, it was once acquired in exchange for 2 bell tents and a remould tyre for a Bedford van!

About Sunbeam Motor Company

Founded in Wolverhampton in 1899, Sunbeam Motor Car Company was one of Britain's most prestigious automotive manufacturers. The company was renowned for quality engineering, sporting achievements, and multiple land speed records.

Notable Achievement: In 1927, the Sunbeam 1000 HP set a land speed record of 203.79 mph, showcasing the company's engineering excellence.

Driving Experience

The driving position offers an excellent view along the long bonnet through a shallow windscreen. The large 2916cc engine combined with the comparatively light body makes the car remarkably flexible — it will accelerate in third gear from walking pace and cruise at 15mph in top gear without complaint.
